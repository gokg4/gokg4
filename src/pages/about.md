# <span class="text-gradient">Hi there, I'm Gokul!</span> <span class="wave">👋</span>

### Technical Product Manager | SaaS Founder | Indie Hacker

I’m a technical product manager and entrepreneur based in **Chennai, India**. After years of working in the IT industry specializing in enterprise integration and Cloud Engineering, I have transitioned to building my own products full-time.

I am the Founder of **[Ekarna Interactive](https://ekarnainteractive.com/)**, a hybrid product studio focused on rapid SaaS development and shipping AI-native tools for the Indian market.

---

### 🚀 Flagship Products

#### 💌 **[Vivah Invite Studio](https://app.vivahinvite.com/)**
An advanced, AI-powered platform for generating customized digital wedding invitations and managing guest experiences.
* **The Mission:** Eliminating wedding planning friction with multi-resolution AI design generation, Smart Itineraries with Google Places integration, and a dedicated Guest CRM with WhatsApp RSVP.
* **The Stack:** React 19+, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Supabase, Golang.

#### 💍 **[VivahSphere](https://vivahsphere.com/planning-os/)**
My AI-Native Wedding Planning OS—a comprehensive platform acting as a deterministic creative ledger.
* **The Mission:** Merging transactional logistics with creative design through offline-first PWA architecture and Google Gemini-powered conversational AI planners.
* **The Stack:** React 19+, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Supabase.

#### 🏗️ **Ekarna Interactive**
My incorporated hybrid product studio where I build and ship B2B/B2C SaaS solutions.
* **Focus:** Identifying B2B/B2C gaps in the Indian event market and creating robust B2B2C growth loops.
* **Methodology:** Rapid prototyping with LLMs, "Build in Public," and user-centric design.
