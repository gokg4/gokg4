# Hi there, I'm Gokul! 👋

### Cloud Engineer | SaaS Founder | Indie Hacker

I’m a developer and entrepreneur based in **Chennai, India**. After years of working in the IT industry and specializing in Cloud Engineering, I have transitioned to building my own products full-time.

I am currently establishing a **Product Studio** focused on rapid SaaS development and shipping useful tools for the Indian market.

---

### 🚀 Currently Building

#### 💍 **VivahSphere**
My flagship SaaS product—a comprehensive platform to manage the chaotic beauty of Indian Weddings.
* **The Mission:** Simplifying collaboration between families, planners, and vendors.
* **The Stack:** React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Supabase.

#### 🏗️ **Product Studio**
I am setting up a studio structure to build and ship MVPs rapidly.
* **Focus:** Identifying B2B/B2C gaps in the Indian market.
* **Methodology:** Rapid prototyping, "Build in Public," and user-centric design.
